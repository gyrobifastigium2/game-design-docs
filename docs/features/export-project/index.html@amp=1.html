Exploring the Export Project Feature

Imagine the power to transform your entire game project into a professional, beautifully formatted PDF document with a simple click. This capability empowers game developers, both indie creators and seasoned studios, to present their projects in a visually stunning and easily shareable format easier than before.

Pitch Game in Cover Letter

Your project's vision is the heart and soul of your game. Front and center should be your overview for the game. The PDF Export includes a cover letter section where your project description can be used as a compelling game vision statement. This statement encapsulates the essence of your project, ensuring that every reader understands the passion and creativity driving your game development journey.

Game Elements

Game Elements are the building blocks of your game - characters, items, quests, levels, and more. The export will list all these elements comprehensively. You can even include some concept art to give readers a visual sense of your various game elements. Each element is tagged with their categories, making it easy to navigate and understand.

Categories

Creating a structured hierarchy is essential in game development. It lists all your custom categories along with their descriptions. This is useful as a reference for the included Game Elements, ensure your systems are crystal clear to your team and stakeholders.
