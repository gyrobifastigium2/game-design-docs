New Feature: Related Elements panel

  • Eddie Daniels
    • 3 years ago

    We're excited to introduce a new feature that will help make your design efforts easier. With the introduction of the Related Elements panel, we're redefining how you navigate and explore the intricacies of your game elements.

    Effortlessly connect and explore the relationships between different Game Elements, whether it's characters, quests, assets, or mechanics. The Related Elements panel pulls elements that share categories.

    Whether you're a solo indie developer or part of a dynamic game development team, this feature streamlines your workflow, fosters collaboration, and brings clarity to the complexity of game design. It's a tool that not only enhances your creative process but also accelerates your project's progress.
